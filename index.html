Your team, not your tool.

QuidQuo gives your venture its own team of AI agents, led by Aether, your chief of staff. They assess where you stand, build the investment case with you and run the raise. You confirm every step that matters.

Aether leads. The seniors do the work.

You talk to Aether. It briefs the senior agents, hands work between them and brings it back to you. Each senior owns one domain and runs specialists behind it.

Know where you stand, before you pay.

The first assessment is free for every venture. Assessment scores it on desirability, viability, feasibility and impact, and every score traces back to the evidence behind it.

Scored profile

Always. Each dimension with its score, its confidence and the evidence it rests on.

A Finding

When your documents hold up to the gate: one claim particular to your venture, never a template.

Paying never buys a better Finding. It buys more evidence, a stronger team and continuous work as the venture changes.

The investment case, built with you.

Build works through nine sub-agents, one for each part of the case. Your venture profile and data room assemble as you go, and work that goes stale gets flagged.

A raise, modelled the way it behaves.

Deals stall, reverse and die. Deal Flow follows every investor through nine states and records why each one moved, so a pass at pre-seed is a warm contact at Series A.

See your whole team at work.

The Command Centre shows your team, people and agents together: what shipped, what is waiting and what needs you. Step in mid-task and redirect.

Sample activity. Presence turns finished work into drafts you can publish, Market keeps a standing watch, and after the close Investor Relations keeps your holders informed.

You decide. The terms are plain.

You stay in charge

A person confirms every consequential step. Each agent asks first, acts and reports, or acts, as you set it.

Your data stays yours

Using it to improve QuidQuo is a separate yes, off by default, and you can withdraw it.
